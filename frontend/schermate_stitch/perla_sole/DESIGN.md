## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an artisanal Mediterranean summer aesthetic: sun-drenched coastal ease meets contemporary boutique luxury. The experience balances tactile craftsmanship with high-converting mobile commerce efficiency. It targets design-conscious Gen Z and millennial tastemakers who seek personal, handmade jewelry imbued with warmth, playful color palettes, and elevated curation.

The visual direction merges **warm tactile minimalism** with **editorial sunlit soft-focus**. Surfaces recall textured whitewashed plaster, sunlit terracotta, and sea-tossed sea glass. The interface relies on generous spatial breathing room, pill-shaped interactions, and fluid micro-gestures. The emotional register is sunlit, optimistic, effortlessly chic, and tactile.

## Colors

The color palette captures sun-bleached ceramics, mineral stone, and vibrant beaded accents:

- **Primary (`#E85D4E`)**: A sun-baked terracotta coral. Used decisively for primary conversions, active filter states, cart highlights, and vital promotional focal points.
- **Secondary (`#7B9E8F`)**: A calm, saline sage green. Represents coastal foliage, organic materials, inventory stock indicators, and supportive interactive triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#F4C582`)**: Warm amber sand. Used for warm badge fills, star reviews, seasonal spotlights, and pearlescent gradients.
- **Neutral (`#2B2623`)**: Espresso charcoal. Replaces harsh blacks for editorial body copy, crisp iconography, and high-legibility typographic anchors.

### Supporting Surfaces & Shimmer Tones
- **Base Canvas (`#FAF8F5`)**: Cream alabaster, offering an organic alternative to sterile white.
- **Surface Tint (`#FFF1EB`)**: Blushed peach for secondary product cards, selected item backdrops, and promotional banners.
- **Pearl Shimmer (`linear-gradient(135deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #F5F1EB 50%, #FFEFE8 100%)`)**: Multi-tonal luminescence for premium tiers, bespoke unboxing cards, and exclusive drops.

## Typography

The typographical pairing reflects vacation-inspired editorial storytelling alongside effortless mobile scannability:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys classical Mediterranean craftsmanship and luxury boutique poise. Used for collection titles, evocative campaign banners, and high-impact hero statements.
- **Body & Functional UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Provides clarity, youthful optimism, and open letter counters. Used for product titles, pricing, descriptions, review text, and checkout flows.
- **Labels & Micro-copy (`Plus Jakarta Sans Medium/Bold`)**: Sized and spaced specifically to keep product tags, bead size selections, and badge labels readable on mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first fluid layout standard tailored for thumb-driven interactions, vertical feeds, and dual-column merchandise grids:

- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `margin-sm` (16px) or `margin` (20px), and `gutter-sm` (12px). Dual-column product arrangements allow maximum visual presence for intricate bead photography without edge crowding.
- **Tablet (640px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `gutter` (16px) and `margin` (24px). Product listings reflow to 3 or 4 columns.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with a fixed maximum container of `1240px`, flanked by `margin-lg` (40px) and `gutter-lg` (24px).

Vertical rhythm follows a 4px/8px modular base scale, pairing tight groupings (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) inside card modules with relaxed spacing (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) between editorial collections.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system shuns harsh black shadows in favor of warm, sunlit atmospheric diffusion:

- **Base Surfaces**: Flat planes grounded in warm cream (`#FAF8F5`) with selective framing via peach and sand tonals.
- **Resting Elevation (Cards & Carousels)**: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(135, 102, 85, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(135, 102, 85, 0.04)`. A gentle terracotta-tinted glow that floats product cards effortlessly.
- **Active / Drag Elevation (Modals, Sliders, Sticky Bag Bar)**: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(92, 60, 43, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(92, 60, 43, 0.05)`.
- **Glassmorphic Overlays**: Applied to floating sticky headers, sheet backdrops, and bottom navigation ribbons using `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.82)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(14px) saturate(160%)`.

## Shapes

The shape language is rounded, playful, and organic—echoing the silhouettes of baroque freshwater pearls, smooth sea glass, and tumbled ceramic beads:

- **Outer Enclosures & Primary Cards**: Generous curvature (`24px` to `28px` / `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`) creating smooth, pebble-like surfaces.
- **Controls & CTAs**: Fully pill-shaped geometries (`9999px`) for buttons, search pills, and chips, making touch targets intuitive and friendly.
- **Media Frames**: Product photography utilizes soft inner masks (`20px`) with optional asymmetrical organic cutouts for featured editorial collages.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), `#E85D4E` background with `#FAF8F5` typography, 52px mobile height for thumb accessibility, and micro-scale response (`scale(0.97)`) on tap.
- **Secondary CTA**: Pill shape with `#FFF1EB` background, `#E85D4E` label, and an ultra-subtle border `1px solid rgba(232, 93, 78, 0.15)`.
- **Ghost / Editorial Action**: Playfair Display italic underlined with subtle terracotta stroke, zero horizontal padding.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Pill-shaped tags with `body-sm` weight. Inactive state: `#FAF8F5` surface with `1px solid #EFEAE3` border and neutral charcoal text. Active state: `#2B2623` background with white text, or `#FFF1EB` surface with `#E85D4E` text and border.

### Product Cards
- Contained within smooth `rounded-2xl` boundaries, with a `#FFFFFF` or `#FFF1EB` tinted base.
- Aspect ratio 4:5 image container with subtle pearl-shimmer placeholder loading skeletons.
- Floating quick-add button (circular 40px pill) pinned to the bottom-right corner of the image with a plus icon.
- Badges (e.g., "Handmade", "Freshwater Pearl", "Only 2 Left") float top-left as miniature translucent pill badges (`label-sm`).

### Form Controls (Inputs, Checkboxes, Radios)
- **Input Fields**: 50px pill or soft `rounded-xl` inputs with `#FFFFFF` background, inset border `1.5px solid #EAE3DA`, and focused glow tint `rgba(232, 93, 78, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 22px rounded organic geometry. Selected state fills with `#7B9E8F` (sage) or `#E85D4E` (coral) with crisp white check/pip.

### Bespoke Jewelry Brand Components
- **Bead Customizer Segment**: Circular tactile swatch buttons with dual-ring selection states for bead pattern sequencing.
- **Material Authenticity Badge**: Dual-ring seal highlighting genuine freshwater pearls and 14k gold-filled hardware, rendered in pearl-sheen gradient fill.
- **Floating Cart Drawer Tab**: Glassmorphic bottom-docked pill displaying total items and direct express checkout.
